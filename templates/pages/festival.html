Know before you go

FAQ.

Is it all ages?

Yes until 8pm. After 8pm the beer garden is 21+.

Can I bring a chair?

Low-back chairs and blankets are welcome. No tents or umbrellas.

Is there re-entry?

Yes. Keep your wristband on.

What if it rains?

The show goes on. Lightning pauses sets until it clears.

Do not miss the early price.

Weekend passes go up $15 on May 1.

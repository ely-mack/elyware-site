WHAT IT IS

A simple live video mixer for two sources.

ELYWARE Video Mixer lets you blend videos and still images live from two decks. It is built for visual performances, projector loops, installations and small events where a full broadcast suite is more than you need.

It is a live playback and mixing tool, not a video editor, recorder or streaming service.

Load, cue, mix and output without setting up scenes or sending your files to the cloud.

HOW IT WORKS

Folder to projector in three moves.

  1. 1

    Load two folders

    Drag videos or image folders onto Deck A and Deck B. Reorder, loop or shuffle each playlist.

  2. 2

    Choose the mix

    Pick a transition and use the crossfader, Auto Mix or Autopilot to move between decks.

  3. 3

    Open the output

    Move the clean output window to a second display or projector and take it full screen, or cast it to a Screenfolk screen over Wi-Fi (pilot).

WIRELESS OUTPUT · SCREENFOLK PARTNERSHIP · PILOT

Mix live. Cast wirelessly. Skip the cable run.

ELYWARE Video Mixer output casts to TVs running the Screenfolk player. Mix from a laptop anywhere in the room. The TV, projector or video wall shows only the finished feed. No HDMI run across the floor. No capture card.

Screenfolk is digital signage software built in Greeley, Colorado. It turns a TV into a remotely managed screen with a browser player or a Fire TV player. Live casting from Video Mixer is in pilot with Screenfolk-managed screens at bars, venues and events.

QUICK ANSWERS

Common questions

What is ELYWARE Video Mixer?

ELYWARE Video Mixer is a free two-deck live video mixer for Mac, Windows and Linux. It plays local videos, images and royalty-free stock clips, blends them with 31 transitions and sends the finished mix to a projector, a second display or, in pilot, a Screenfolk digital signage screen.

Can I send the video mix to a TV wirelessly?

Yes, in pilot. Through the Screenfolk partnership, Video Mixer output casts to a TV running the Screenfolk player on the same network. No HDMI cable or capture card is needed. Screenfolk is digital signage software from Greeley, Colorado. Email hello@elyware.net to join the pilot.

Is Video Mixer free?

Yes. Video Mixer v4.1 is a free public beta. No account, no watermark, no app telemetry.

What do I need to run it?

Mac users need macOS 11 or later and get a self-contained universal app. Windows and Linux users need Python 3 and a modern browser. Full install steps are on the Video Mixer page.

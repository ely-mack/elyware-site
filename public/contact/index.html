03 / Questions

Common questions.

What does ELYWARE build?

Custom AI tools and automations, websites and marketing campaigns, and business apps. We also make free creative software and browser games.

What does custom AI mean here?

Practical AI inside your workflow. Assistants that answer from your own documents, automations that move data between the systems you already use, and reports that build themselves on schedule.

Do you only work with music and entertainment businesses?

No. Our roots are in live music, venues and hospitality. We also build for restaurants, local service businesses and any team with a problem software can solve.

How is a custom project priced?

Every project is scoped individually. Send us what you want to build and your timeline. We reply with a practical next step and a scope that fits.

Can I reach you about Video Mixer or a partnership?

Yes. The same address handles beta feedback, partnerships and support: hello@elyware.net.
